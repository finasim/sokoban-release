Overview

Sokoban ("the App") is a classic crate-pushing puzzle game — you walk around a warehouse grid and push crates onto goal squares. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use it.

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-in, no name or email address is ever requested, and nothing you do in the App is sent anywhere. Every puzzle runs entirely on your device, and the App has no outside data flow at all — no ads, no analytics, no servers.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your progress and preferences locally on your device (using the platform's standard local storage) so they persist between launches:

  • Which levels you have completed, your best move and push counts for each, and the recorded solution used to replay a completed level
  • How many attempts you have made on each level, which is what unlocks later levels
  • Selected theme (Light / Dark / System)
  • Your on-screen control preferences: dial or d-pad, its size, and its horizontal and vertical position
  • Whether sound effects are on
  • Whether haptics (vibration) are on

None of this leaves your device or is linked to you personally. It is written to the App's own private storage and is deleted permanently when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. You can also clear your progress at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

What the App does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No network access at all. The released App does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of sending data off your device.

Permissions

The released App requests no data-access permissions. It does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permission. The only entry in its Android manifest is DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, which Android's own support libraries add automatically — a signature-level permission scoped to this App that grants no access to your data.

Children's privacy

The App is a puzzle game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either, and there is nothing about a child that could be transmitted or shared.

Data security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no database of user information — held by the developer or anyone else — to secure. Your progress and settings are protected by your device's own app-sandboxing and whatever screen lock you have set.
