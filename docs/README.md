## About

Sokoban is the classic warehouse puzzle: push every crate onto a goal tile.

You can only push, never pull, and only one crate at a time — so a single
careless shove can wedge a crate into a corner and cost you the level. Undo is
always one tap away.

## Features

- **100 levels across five packs** — Starter, Depot, Warehouse, Foundry and
  Labyrinth, each pack strictly harder than the last. The first level is a
  single push; the last will take you a while.
- **Every level is solvable.** Each one ships with a known optimal solution,
  verified before release — no level is ever a dead end because of a mistake in
  the puzzle itself.
- **Play the way you like.** Swipe anywhere on the board, or use the on-screen
  controls — a four-way pad or a drag dial, whichever you prefer. Either can be
  resized and moved to wherever your thumb actually sits.
- **Moves and pushes counted separately**, with your best of each saved per
  level — the shortest walk and the fewest pushes are rarely the same solution.
- **A stuck-crate warning** that offers an undo rather than ending your game.
- **Light and dark themes**, following your system setting by default.
- **Sound effects and haptics**, both of which you can turn off.
- **Fully offline.** No account, no sign-in, nothing to load.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing.

There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that
collect data. The released app does not request the Android `INTERNET`
permission at all, so it is not technically capable of sending anything off your
device. Your progress and settings are stored in the app's own private storage
and are deleted when you uninstall it.

📄 **[Read the full privacy policy](https://finasim.github.io/sokoban-release/privacypolicy.html)**
